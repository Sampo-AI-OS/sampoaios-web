    | 3. MULTI-MODAL EVENT INGESTION & NLP SENTIMENT VECTORIZATION

    3.1 Streaming Architecture and Ingestion Pipeline

    To satisfy institutional low-latency requirements, the platform ingests heterogeneous unstructured data feeds through a distributed, event-driven Apache Kafka pipeline:

  • Diplomatic & Open-Source Wire Feeds: Continuous ingestion of global press releases, diplomatic cables, and international news wire feeds (Reuters, Bloomberg, AP).
  • Sovereign Sanctions & Regulatory Feeds: Direct polling of the US Department of the Treasury (OFAC Specially Designated Nationals), EU Consolidated Sanctions, and UN Security Council resolution logs.
  • Maritime AIS Satellite Telemetry: Automatic Identification System (AIS) transponder feeds capturing live vessel traffic density, speed over ground, and navigational heading across global maritime bottlenecks.
  • Disaster & Crisis APIs: Integration with the UN/JRC Global Disaster Alert and Coordination System (GDACS) RSS endpoints, ingesting natural hazard, conflict, and humanitarian crisis alerts.

    • 3.2 Semantic Entity Extraction and Event Classification

    Incoming text articles are stripped of rhetorical noise and vectorized using an optimized domain-specific transformer model. The pipeline extracts a normalized event state tuple:

    $$\mathcal{E} = \langle t_{\text{detected}}, \mathbf{x}_{\text{geo}}, \text{Type}, S, \mathcal{C}, \boldsymbol{\kappa}_{\text{affected}} \rangle$$

    where:

  • $t_{\text{detected}}$ is a UTC ISO-8601 monotonic microsecond timestamp.
  • $\mathbf{x}_{\text{geo}} = (\text{lat}, \text{lon}, \text{ISO-3166})$ represents the physical coordinate of the incident.
  • $\text{Type} \in \{\text{armed\_conflict}, \text{sanctions}, \text{shipping\_disruption}, \text{market\_shock}, \text{geopolitical\_tension}, \text{regulation}\}$.
  • $S \in [1, 10]$ is an integer severity grading representing conflict intensity, troop mobilization, or trade volume affected.
  • $\mathcal{C} \in [0.0, 1.0]$ is the Bayesian confidence score of the extracted intelligence.
  • $\boldsymbol{\kappa}_{\text{affected}}$ is a vector of affected industrial sectors (Energy, Semiconductors, Aerospace, Agriculture, Shipping).

    • Empirical Severity Grading Metric ($S$)

    The severity rating is deterministically evaluated using a multi-factor rubric:

    $$S = \min \left( 10, \left\lfloor 1.5 \cdot \mathcal{F}_{\text{kinetic}} + 2.0 \cdot \mathcal{F}_{\text{chokepoint}} + 1.2 \cdot \mathcal{F}_{\text{trade\_vol}} + 1.0 \cdot \mathcal{F}_{\text{nuclear/escalation}} \right\rfloor \right)$$

    where $\mathcal{F}_{\text{kinetic}} \in \{0, 1, 2\}$ indicates military engagement, $\mathcal{F}_{\text{chokepoint}} \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ indicates direct proximity to strategic shipping bottlenecks, and $\mathcal{F}_{\text{trade\_vol}} \in [0, 2]$ reflects global trade share impacted.
